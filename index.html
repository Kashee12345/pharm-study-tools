Pharmacology · Modules 1 to 15

Pharmacology Study Tools

See how drugs work. Each module has an animated walkthrough that shows each drug class acting at its target, so the effects, side effects and nursing checks make sense instead of being memorized, plus a Lab with simulators, patient cases and sorting games. Everything runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No login, nothing graded.

How to use this: open the walkthrough first and go at your own pace with the arrow keys or Next. Then open the Lab: move the sliders, give the drugs, and work the patient cases. Use the tools bar at the top of each page to pause the animations, slow them down, have the text read aloud, or get a written description of each picture. For learning only.
